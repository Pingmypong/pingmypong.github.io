Ping My Pong.

Hall of Fame

Everyone who returns 75 balls in a row in Ping My Pong goes in the Hall of Fame, at the very top of this website. The first one keeps the #1 spot forever. Here's how to get in.

Who runs it

It's run by Brainrot Games, the developer of Ping My Pong. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, the Hall of Fame in any way.

How to get in

  1. Return 75 balls in a row in a single Arcade run (not the daily challenge).
  2. Play signed in to Game Center, so the score reaches the global "Best run" leaderboard.
  3. Email danielgo.carrillo+pingmypong@gmail.com with your Game Center name and that run's "Challenge a friend" card, or a screenshot of the game-over screen.

You don't need to record your runs. Everyone who does all three gets in, in the order they arrive. Using the in-game second chance is allowed.

What you get

  • Your name in the Hall of Fame, the first thing on this website, for good.
  • The first one to get there keeps the #1 spot, and nobody takes it.
  • A video about your run on the game's social accounts, if you want one.

There's no cash prize, and no purchase is needed to take part: the game is free.

Checking

Before announcing anyone, we check that the score shows up on the global Game Center leaderboard under your name and that you've unlocked the "The Wall" achievement. If something doesn't add up, we may ask for extra proof, like a short video of you playing. Scores made with cheats, modified versions or outside tools don't count. Brainrot Games' decision is final.

Your name

We only publish your name with your permission, and you choose how it appears (your Game Center name or another nickname).

How long

Open since October 2026, with no end date. If the rules change, this page is updated.
