The Wall Bounty

The first player to return 75 balls in a row in Ping My Pong gets their name on The Wall. Here are the rules.

Who runs it

It's run by Brainrot Games, the developer of Ping My Pong. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, this bounty in any way.

How to win

  1. Return 75 balls in a row in a single Arcade run (not the daily challenge).
  2. The run has to show up on the global "Best run" Game Center leaderboard.
  3. Send a screen recording of the run and your Game Center name to danielgo.carrillo+pingmypong@gmail.com.

The first person to do all three wins. Using the in-game second chance is allowed.

The prize

  • Your name in the Hall of Fame on this website, for good.
  • Your name on the game's App Store page.
  • A video about your run on the game's social accounts.

There's no cash prize, and no purchase is needed to take part: the game is free.

Checking

We check the recording and the Game Center leaderboard before announcing anyone. Scores made with cheats, modified versions or outside tools don't count. Brainrot Games' decision is final.

Your name

We only publish your name with your permission, and you choose how it appears (your Game Center name or another nickname).

Dates

It starts in October 2026 and ends when there's a verified winner. If the rules change, this page is updated.
